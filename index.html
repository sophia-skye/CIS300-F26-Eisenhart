! Updates !

11/13/2026 The new Hunger Games movie; The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is out next week and, like the other movies in the series, has a brand new soundtrack full of popular artists and original songs!

The lead single for the movie was Noah Kahan's "Draw You Out." Other singles included original songs from artists like The Smashing Pumpkins, Gigi Perez, and Lizzy McAlpine!

The full soundtrack was just released today, containing a total of 29 tracks from a variety of artists. The soundtrack has a variety of sounds on it, from simple vocal renditions of poems to harder hitting folk-rock songs.

I really enjoyed the variety on the album and am all the more excited to see the movie after listening to it!
