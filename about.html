About



There is nothing better than discovering an album that you can listen to over, and over again

Before this year, I felt like I was only a "song listener," and that I hadn't listened to a lot of albums or artists from outside of my "norm." So this year I made it my goal to listen to more songs, more artists, more albums, and more genres! I made this website to be able to converse with a wider range of people about the music they love and to keep track of my progress!

What kind of music do I like?

I love music across all genres: rock, pop, rap, r&b, jazz, dance, country, folk, and many many more. I don't consider myself picky when it comes to the music I like. The most important thing about music, to me, is that it accompanies a moment. A soft, acoustic folk song while watching the rain, an arrangement of upbeat synths to dance with friends, a feel-good pop song to blast in the car, an emotional ballad to cry along to. I love it all!





Who am I?

My name is Sophia and I am a 19 year-old junior at Arizona State University. I am currently pursuing a degree in Media Arts and Sciences! I was born and raised in Phoenix and, although I hate the heat, I do really love the sunsets here.

I have always loved listening to music. My dad blasting music throughout the house at 6 am and 2013 ALT AZ 93.3 FM were both very formative to my musical upbringing. In my free time I love crocheting, drawing, playing volleyball, and playing guitar! If I am not answering my phone, I am probably watching a 2000's teen drama with my sister.
