About



There is nothing better than discovering an album that you can listen to over, and over again

Before this year, I felt like I never really explored different kinds of music. One of my goals at the beginning of this year was to listen to more music. I was only a "song listener," I hadn't listened to a lot of albums or artists from outside my "norm." So, this year, I made it my goal to listen to more music. More songs, more artists, more albums, and more genres!


What kind of music do I like?

I love music across all genres: rock, pop, rap, r&b, jazz, dance, country, folk, and many many more. I don't consider myself picky when it comes to the music I like. The most important thing about music, to me, is that it accompanies a moment. A soft, acoustic folk song while watching the rain, an arrangement of upbeat synths to dance with friends, a feel-good pop song to blast in the car, an emotional ballad to cry along to. I love it all. Music is one of the most important forms of expression. I am very excited to share my love for music with you!
